# Replace existing FDDs with Version 1

## What will change
Use the attached **2026 Neuron Garage FDD w Exhibits Nov 2026.pdf** as **FDD Version 1** for future staff emails and prospect downloads.

The PDF has 121 pages. Its cover states an investment of **$8,285–$14,000** and an issuance date of **March 1, 2026**. We will save that printed date, not infer a November issuance date from its filename. The complete original PDF will be uploaded without edits; the document reader reviewed only the first 50 pages.

## Important finding and deletion safeguard
There are four existing FDD uploads. Three have no linked send, receipt, or compliance records. One test-named FDD has two compliance records, nine delivery events, one email-send record, and one signed receipt.

Deleting that linked file would remove evidence of what someone received and signed. This plan therefore permanently deletes the three unused files and removes the linked file from active choices, while keeping its original file and records privately for the audit trail. This is a deliberate exception to deleting every old file. It will not be offered for new sends or latest-FDD downloads.

No past receipt, delivery date, signature, or agreement date will be rewritten to claim that the new Version 1 was sent earlier.

## Phase 1 — Replace and verify (estimated 1–2 Lovable turns)
1. Upload the entire attachment to the existing protected FDD storage. Name it clearly as Version 1 and save its printed issuance date and file fingerprint.
2. Confirm the stored PDF matches the attachment before removing anything.

4. Make Version 1 the only active FDD in Portal Files, the staff FDD version picker, and the prospect latest-FDD download.
5. Check that future FDD emails use Version 1. Do not send any real prospect an email during this replacement.
6. Test download and send-selection behavior, and confirm the old receipt and audit report still resolve their original version. Report any live email check that remains untested.

- Permanent deletion of unused files cannot be undone.

- Prospects who already received the old file keep that version on their receipt. A new send must be recorded honestly if staff later sends them Version 1.
- Keep existing prospect access checks, receipt signing rules, waiting periods, and email delivery proof unchanged.
- Do not touch other portal homework, forms, call scheduling, City Search, Teacher Search, or Email Outreach.
- No new version system or broader page redesign is planned. Use the current upload, version selection, and audit system.
